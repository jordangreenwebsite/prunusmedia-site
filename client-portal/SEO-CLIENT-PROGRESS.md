# Per-business keywords and client progress
The shared portal navigation adds Keywords & SEO progress to every authorised website business, including mobile and business overview. The primary registered domain of each business is the tracking scope; additional domains under the same business are not independent tracking projects.

Agency can add saved keywords, select up to 50 existing keywords to configure rank targets with explicit location/device and optional paid weekly monitoring, pause and enable existing checks, and request a manual paid check. The full SEO command centre remains agency-only. The keyword page shows up to 15 open evidence-based non-blog recommendations for the selected business, with explicit completion confirmation. No tasks are automatically completed.

Clients see only up to five explicitly selected focus keywords from their business (none until selected), registered-domain target page, measured organic rank, location/device, check date, month movement, and matching 28-day Search Console query metrics. No new paid requests or provider calls occur through the read-only progress API. Monthly movement compares the latest current-month observation with the last observation from the previous calendar month or the first current-month observation; a missing position is never treated as rank 31. A keyword counts as improved if at least one comparable target improves this month. Weekly enabled is a schedule setting, not proof that a check has run. Search Console monthly totals include saved reported days only; compare matching reported day numbers against the previous month, and label missing data. Client views do not expose provider spend, competitor data, raw GSC query lists, credentials, internal action evidence, or another client's data.

Completed-work updates are manually reviewed and explicitly shared by an admin; optional link to a completed internal recommendation, or manual note. They can be removed from the client view; no client email is sent. They are not an automatic claim of ranking improvement. Migrations 0013 and 0014 are additive. The agency selects up to five focus keywords in dedicated slots per business; changes replace the selection atomically, do not stop any tracking, and do not expose private keyword counts or rank details. Client keyword summaries are calculated only from selected terms; website-wide Search Console totals remain clearly labelled. The selection cap is enforced by the API and database. Deleting a keyword frees its reporting slot. Existing businesses begin with no shared keywords rather than automatically disclosing private tracking. Test role isolation, actual rankings and GSC data after deployment. Production deployment and credentials still required.
