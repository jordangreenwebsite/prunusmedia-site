## The project

Easy Cloud Accounting is an independent **Prunus Media website demonstration** built to show what a polished, purpose-built accountancy website can look like. It is not presented as an independently verified paying client website.

## What visitors can explore

The hosted example gives accountancy firms a real website to explore instead of a static concept card. Its presentation focuses on building trust, explaining services and guiding visitors towards making an enquiry.

[Explore the complete Easy Cloud Accounting demo](https://easycloudaccounting.pages.dev/), or see the prominent preview on our [Websites for Accountants](/industries/accountants/) page.

## Important note

The demonstration does not imply real clients, regulatory status, professional qualifications or measured search and conversion results for the example business.
