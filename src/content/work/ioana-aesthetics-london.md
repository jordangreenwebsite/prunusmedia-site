## Independent website concept

This is an **uncommissioned Prunus Media portfolio demonstration**, inspired by the publicly visible service menu and social presence of Ioana Aesthetics London. The clinic has **not** approved, purchased or endorsed the design.

## What the concept demonstrates

A warm ivory and sage visual identity, considered typography, consultation-focused calls to action, eleven individual treatment information pages, four local travel pages, patient-friendly FAQs, pricing context, aftercare guidance and editorial articles. The site is mobile responsive and uses structured metadata and meaningful internal links.

## Important publication notes

The demo uses **illustrative stock photography**; it does not portray the clinic's premises, practitioner or actual patients. Reviews, professional credentials and booking details have not been invented. It is intentionally noindexed until business approval. The preview is not evidence of client acquisition, improved search rankings or conversion results.
